# Handbook: Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

**Owner:** People & Human Resources
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-01
**Source:** Internal Notion — People Handbook

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

- Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of PTO per year.
- PTO accrues monthly and carries over up to **5 days** into the next year.
- Requests should be submitted at least **2 weeks** in advance for 3+ consecutive days.

## Sick Leave

- Separate from PTO: **10 days** per year, not carried over.
- No advance notice required; notify your manager as soon as practical.

## Parental Leave

- **16 weeks** paid for the primary caregiver.
- **8 weeks** paid for the secondary caregiver.
- Must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Leave of Absence

- Employees marked **On Leave** retain benefits and system access per their status.
- Extended unpaid leave beyond 12 weeks requires People Operations approval.

## Approval Workflow

1. Employee submits a request in the HR system.
2. Manager approves or declines within 3 business days.
3. People Operations records the status change.
