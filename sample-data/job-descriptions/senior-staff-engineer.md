# Senior Staff Engineer — Core Infrastructure

**Level:** L6
**Department:** Core Infrastructure
**Reports to:** Director of Engineering

## Role Summary

The Senior Staff Engineer is a technical leader who sets the direction for the
platform's reliability, scalability, and architecture. This role operates across
multiple teams, driving large initiatives that span quarters rather than sprints.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own the architecture of core platform services handling millions of requests per day.
- Lead design reviews and set engineering standards across the Infrastructure org.
- Mentor senior and staff engineers; grow the technical bench.
- Partner with product and SRE leadership on capacity planning and incident prevention.
- Drive cross-team initiatives such as multi-region failover and zero-downtime deploys.

## Required Background

- 10+ years of software engineering experience, with 4+ in distributed systems.
- Deep expertise in at least one of: Go, Rust, Java, or Python.
- Proven track record operating services at scale on AWS or equivalent cloud.
- Strong grounding in observability, SLOs, and reliability engineering.

## Everyday Expectations

- Spend roughly 50% of time on hands-on technical work and 50% on leadership.
- Review critical pull requests and unblock teams within one business day.
- Represent engineering in architecture and planning forums.
