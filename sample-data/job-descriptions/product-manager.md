# Product Manager — Product

**Level:** L5
**Department:** Product
**Reports to:** Group Product Manager

## Role Summary

The Product Manager owns the roadmap for a product area, translating customer
needs and business goals into prioritized, shippable work. They are the connective
tissue between engineering, design, data, and go-to-market teams.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and maintain the product roadmap for an assigned area.
- Write clear product requirement documents and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize the backlog based on customer impact, effort, and strategic fit.
- Partner with design and engineering through discovery, delivery, and launch.
- Analyze adoption and retention metrics to inform the next iteration.

## Required Background

- 4+ years of product management experience in a software company.
- Comfort with data: SQL, dashboards, and A/B test interpretation.
- Strong written and verbal communication across technical and non-technical audiences.

## Everyday Expectations

- Run weekly backlog refinement and sprint planning with the engineering team.
- Maintain a public roadmap and communicate changes to stakeholders.
- Own launch readiness: documentation, enablement, and success metrics.
